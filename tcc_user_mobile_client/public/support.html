Frequently Asked Questions

Accounts

How do I create an account?

Download the TCC app, tap "Sign Up", and follow the prompts to enter your email, phone number, and create a password.

How do I reset my password?

Tap "Forgot Password" on the login screen, enter your email, and follow the instructions sent to your inbox.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Note that this action is permanent and all data will be removed.

Payments

What payment methods are supported?

We support credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express) and bank transfers.

Why was my payment declined?

Payments may be declined due to insufficient funds, incorrect card details, or bank restrictions. Please verify your information or contact your bank.

How do I add money to my wallet?

Go to Wallet > Add Money, enter the amount, select your payment method, and confirm the transaction.

KYC (Know Your Customer)

Why do I need to verify my identity?

Identity verification is required by financial regulations to prevent fraud and ensure the security of all users.

What documents are accepted for KYC?

We accept government-issued photo IDs such as passports, driver's licenses, and national ID cards.

How long does KYC verification take?

Most verifications are completed within 24-48 hours. You'll receive a notification once your verification is complete.

Transfers

How do I send money to another user?

Go to Transfer > Send Money, enter the recipient's phone number or email, specify the amount, and confirm.

What are the transfer limits?

Transfer limits depend on your verification level. Verified users can transfer up to $10,000 per day.

How long do transfers take?

Internal transfers are instant. Bank withdrawals typically take 1-3 business days.
