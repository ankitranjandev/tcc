Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your information

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

TCC - The Community Coin ("TCC," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application and services.

By using TCC, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

2. Personal Data We Collect

2.1 Identity and Contact Information

We collect personal information that you provide when registering for an account:

  • Full Name: Your first and last name for account identification and verification
  • Email Address: For account login, notifications, and communication
  • Phone Number: For account verification, OTP authentication, and security alerts
  • Date of Birth: To verify you meet age requirements
  • Nationality: For regulatory compliance purposes
  • Profile Picture: Optional profile photo for account personalization

2.2 Financial Data

To provide our financial services, we collect and process:

  • Bank Account Details: Bank name, account number, and routing information for withdrawals
  • Payment Card Information: Card details processed securely through Stripe for deposits
  • Transaction History: Records of all deposits, withdrawals, transfers, and investments
  • Wallet Balance: Your current TCC wallet balance and transaction amounts
  • Investment Records: Details of your investments including amounts, terms, and returns

2.3 KYC (Know Your Customer) Documents

For regulatory compliance and identity verification, we collect:

  • Government-Issued ID: Front and back images of your national ID, passport, or driver's license
  • Selfie Photograph: A clear photo of your face for identity verification matching
  • Proof of Address: Utility bills or bank statements when required

Important: KYC documents are stored securely and used solely for identity verification purposes. Access is strictly limited to authorized personnel.

2.4 Device and Usage Information

We automatically collect:

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Features used, time spent in app, interaction patterns
  • Location Data: Approximate location for agent finder features (only when permitted)
  • Log Data: IP address, access times, app crashes and errors

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

Core Services:
  • Create and manage your TCC account
  • Process deposits, withdrawals, and transfers
  • Facilitate investments and bill payments
  • Verify your identity (KYC compliance)
  • Provide customer support
  • Security: Detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and illegal activities
  • Communication: Send transaction confirmations, security alerts, and service updates
  • Improvement: Analyze usage patterns to improve our services and user experience
  • Legal Compliance: Meet regulatory requirements and respond to legal requests

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We share your information with trusted third parties who assist in operating our services. We do not sell your personal information.

4.1 Stripe (Payment Processing)

We use Stripe, Inc. for secure payment processing:

  • Data Shared: Name, email, payment card details, billing address
  • Purpose: Processing card payments for wallet deposits
  • Privacy Policy: stripe.com/privacy

Stripe is PCI-DSS Level 1 certified, the highest level of payment security compliance. Your card details are processed directly by Stripe and are not stored on our servers.

4.2 Firebase (Google Cloud Platform)

We use Firebase services provided by Google:

  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication and session management
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notifications for transaction alerts and updates
  • Firebase Analytics: Anonymous app usage analytics for service improvement
  • Firebase Crashlytics: Error reporting to improve app stability
  • Privacy Policy: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4.3 Amazon Web Services (AWS)

Our infrastructure is hosted on AWS:

  • Data Storage: Secure encrypted storage of user data and documents
  • Content Delivery: Fast, secure delivery of app content via CloudFront
  • Privacy Policy: aws.amazon.com/privacy

4.4 KYC Verification Services

We may use third-party identity verification services to validate your identity documents and comply with regulatory requirements.

5. Data Security

We implement robust security measures to protect your information:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted is encrypted using SSL/TLS protocols (HTTPS)
  • Secure Storage: Personal data is stored in encrypted databases with access controls
  • Access Controls: Strict role-based access limits who can access your data
  • OTP Verification: Sensitive transactions require one-time password verification
  • Token-Based Authentication: Secure JWT tokens with automatic expiration
  • Regular Audits: Security assessments and vulnerability testing

Important: While we strive to protect your personal information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide our services to you
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Transaction records and financial data may be retained for up to 7 years for regulatory compliance. KYC documents are retained for the duration of your account plus any legally required period.

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Right to Correction: Update or correct inaccurate information in your account settings or by contacting us
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your account and data (subject to legal retention requirements). You can initiate account deletion from the Account screen in the app.
  • Right to Portability: Receive your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Right to Object: Object to certain types of data processing
  • Right to Restrict Processing: Request limitation of how we use your data

To exercise your rights:

  • Update profile information directly in the app
  • Delete your account from Account Settings
  • Contact us at privacy@tcc-app.com for other requests

We will respond to your request within 30 days.

8. Legal Disclosure

We may disclose your information when required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal obligations, court orders, and regulatory requirements
  • Protect and defend our rights and property
  • Prevent fraud, money laundering, or other illegal activities
  • Protect the safety of our users and the public
  • Respond to requests from law enforcement agencies

9. Children's Privacy

TCC is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected data from a child under 18, we will take steps to delete such information promptly.

10. International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than Sierra Leone, including the United States and the European Union where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers in compliance with applicable data protection laws.

11. Push Notifications

With your consent, we may send push notifications for:

  • Transaction confirmations and alerts
  • Security notifications (login attempts, password changes)
  • Investment updates and maturity reminders
  • Important service announcements

You can disable push notifications in your device settings or app preferences at any time.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:

  • Posting the new Privacy Policy on this page
  • Updating the "Last Updated" date
  • Sending you an email or in-app notification for significant changes

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of TCC after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Regulatory Compliance

TCC operates in compliance with applicable financial regulations in Sierra Leone, including:

  • Anti-Money Laundering (AML) requirements
  • Know Your Customer (KYC) regulations
  • Bank of Sierra Leone guidelines
  • Data protection requirements
